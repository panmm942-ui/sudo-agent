# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The summary below is binding; the linked text is the full reference.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Sudo to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent; most people here are giving their free time.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Keep bug reports factual and reproducible.
- Respect people's privacy: don't post others' personal data, account details or private messages.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal and political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public space where
someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/panmm942-ui/sudo-agent/security/advisories/new)
(mark the report as a conduct issue) or by contacting [@panmm942-ui](https://github.com/panmm942-ui) on GitHub.
All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may, depending on severity: correct a comment, issue a private or public warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban someone from the project's spaces — following
the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
